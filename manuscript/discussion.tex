\subsection{Discussion}

The Jev-like organization turns a screen of a library against many pockets into a matrix product. On one \tmAGpu{} the dual encoder ranks all \tmALig{} ligands of DOCKSTRING against 57 pockets from SMILES in \tmADualFiftySevenFull{} s, of which \tmAFeat{} s is the featurization of the ligands, and the same ranking against 1{,}000 pockets takes \tmADualThousandFull{} s, while the fastest search mode of the docking engine needs \pocketDockDaysFast{} GPU-days for the 57 pockets. The ligand embedding is computed once for the library, and every further pocket costs a matrix product, so that the cost of the Jev-like networks grows with the number of pockets by \tmADualPerPocketMs{} to \tmAPooledPerPocketMs{} ms per pocket where the cost of a joint network grows by \tmAXaHalfPerPocket{} to \tmAXaSinglePerPocket{} s per pocket. The cross-attention network needs \tmARatioXaHalfThousandModel{} to \tmARatioXaSingleThousandModel{} times the model time of the dual encoder for 1{,}000 pockets, and \tmARatioXaHalfThousandFull{} to \tmARatioXaSingleThousandFull{} times from SMILES to scores. For 57 pockets the end-to-end ratio is only \tmARatioXaHalfFiftySevenFull{} to \tmARatioXaSingleFiftySevenFull{}, because the featurization of the ligands, which every network needs, dominates the time of the dual encoder. The scale at which the organization pays is therefore the screen of a library against hundreds of pockets or more, such as the genome-wide screen of DrugCLIP over about 10{,}000 human proteins,\cite{drugclip} where the ligand embedding is shared by all pockets.

The recall that the organization gives up is a few points of the top-1\% docking hits at a budget of the best 10\% of the ranking. The dual encoder keeps \kfRatioDual{}\% and the pooled network \kfRatioPooled{}\% of the recall of the cross-attention network on the known pockets, and the pocket state adds \kfDiffDualConstTen{} to \kfDiffXattnXattnConstTen{} to the recall of a network that sees only the ligand, so that all three networks use the pocket of their training data. The three networks therefore cover a range from the cheapest first stage, the dual encoder, to the most accurate one, the cross-attention network, and the pooled network has the cost of the dual encoder with \kfDiffPooledDualTen{} more recall when the checkpoint is chosen on known pockets. A shortlist of the best 10\% of the ranking costs a tenth of docking the library, which saves a factor of ten in the docking of the library once the networks are trained, at the recall of Table~\ref{tab:known}, and the best 20\% hold \kfDualTwenty{} to \kfXattnTwenty{} of the hits. The docked training sample of 40{,}000 ligands per pocket is \trainShareLib{}\% of the library, so that the first screen of the library, with the sample and the shortlist docked, costs \shareLabelShortlist{}\% of docking the library, a saving of a factor of \factorLabelShortlist{}, and every further library that the networks screen saves the factor of ten. The choice of the checkpoint matters for the joint network: when the checkpoint is chosen on new targets, the three networks are within \kpSpreadTen{} of each other, and the cross-attention network stops after \kpXaBestLo{} to \kpXaBestHi{} updates against \kfXaBestLo{} to \kfXaBestHi{} updates when the checkpoint is chosen on known pockets.

The networks serve the pockets of their training data. They are trained on the docking scores of the pockets of their training data, with a docked sample of 40{,}000 ligands per pocket (\kfNTargets{} pockets across the folds of this study), and the screens of this study rank new ligands against these known pockets, the setting of a docked training sample for every pocket of interest. For a pocket outside the training data the networks have no target-specific knowledge to apply, and the analyses of the preceding study in the Supporting Information show that the ranking then follows the ligand ranking that the training pockets share, so that a pocket that is not in the training data needs docking scores of its own before it can be screened with this organization. The recall is measured against Vina scores of DOCKSTRING, a ranking is not a docking score, and the shortlist still has to be docked.

DrugCLIP is Jev-like in this sense.\cite{drugclip} PharmacoNet computes the pocket model once and then aligns every ligand conformer to it by graph matching,\cite{pharmaconet} so that its ligand side depends on the pocket, and the speed-ups of 3{,}000 and ten million over docking that the two report refer to other tasks and hardware. The released checkpoints, the timing code and the per-stage records give the same cost measurement for other pocket-conditioned networks on DOCKSTRING, and the alanine-truncation re-dockings of the supporting pocket-edit check (Supporting Information) give a test of how a network responds to an edited pocket. Future work will compare these published networks with the networks of this study on the same pockets and ligands.
