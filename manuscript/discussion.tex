\subsection{Discussion}

The Jev-like organization turns a screen of a library against many pockets into a matrix product. The ligand embedding is computed once for the library, and every further pocket adds one pocket state and one readout. For 57 pockets the featurization of the ligands, which every network needs, dominates the time of the dual encoder, and the advantage over the cross-attention network is small. It grows with the number of pockets. The organization therefore pays in the screen of a library against hundreds of pockets or more, such as the genome-wide screen of DrugCLIP over about 10{,}000 human proteins,\cite{drugclip} where all pockets share the ligand embedding.

The price of the organization is a few points of recall at a budget of the best 10\% of the ranking. The dual encoder keeps \kfRatioDual{}\% of the recall of the cross-attention network, and the best 20\% of the ranking hold \kfDualTwenty{} and \kfXattnTwenty{} of the hits. A shortlist of the best 10\% of the ranking costs a tenth of docking the library, so that the shortlist saves a factor of ten in the docking of the library at the recall of Table~\ref{tab:known}. The docked training sample of 40{,}000 ligands per pocket is \trainShareLib{}\% of the library, so the first screen of the library, with the sample and the shortlist docked, costs \shareLabelShortlist{}\% of docking the library, a saving of a factor of \factorLabelShortlist{}. Every further library that the networks screen saves the factor of ten.

The networks serve the pockets of their training data. They were trained on a docked sample of 40{,}000 ligands per pocket and rank new ligands against these pockets, which is the setting of a screen with a docked sample for every pocket of interest. For a pocket outside the training data the networks have no target-specific knowledge to apply, and the analyses of the preceding study in the Supporting Information show that the ranking then follows the ligand ranking that the training pockets share. Such a pocket needs docking scores of its own before this organization can screen it. The recall is measured against the Vina scores of DOCKSTRING, and the shortlist still has to be docked.

DrugCLIP embeds pockets and molecules independently and is Jev-like in the sense of this study.\cite{drugclip} PharmacoNet computes the pocket model once and then aligns every ligand conformer to it by graph matching,\cite{pharmaconet} so its ligand side depends on the pocket. The speed-ups of ten million and 3{,}000 over docking that DrugCLIP and PharmacoNet report refer to other tasks and hardware. The released checkpoints, the timing code and the per-stage records allow the same cost measurement for other pocket-conditioned networks on DOCKSTRING. Future work will compare these published networks with the networks of this study on the same pockets and ligands.
