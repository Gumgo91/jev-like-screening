\subsection{On known pockets the Jev-like network keeps most of the recall of the joint network}

At a budget of the best 10\% of the ranking, the dual encoder recovers \kfDualTen{} of the top-1\% docking hits of the \kfNTargets{} known pockets (95\% interval \kfDualTenLo{} to \kfDualTenHi{}) and the cross-attention network \kfXattnTen{} (\kfXattnTenLo{} to \kfXattnTenHi{}; Table~\ref{tab:known}). The cross-attention network recovers \kfDiffXattnDualTen{} (\kfDiffXattnDualTenLo{} to \kfDiffXattnDualTenHi{}) more of the hits than the dual encoder, and the dual encoder keeps \kfRatioDual{}\% (\kfRatioDualLo{} to \kfRatioDualHi{}) of its recall. A network with one constant dummy pocket for every target recovers \kfConstTen{} (\kfConstTenLo{} to \kfConstTenHi{}), the recall that the ligand alone supports. The pocket state adds \kfDiffDualConstTen{} (\kfDiffDualConstTenLo{} to \kfDiffDualConstTenHi{}) for the dual encoder and \kfDiffXattnXattnConstTen{} (\kfDiffXattnXattnConstTenLo{} to \kfDiffXattnXattnConstTenHi{}) for the cross-attention network. At the 20\% budget the two networks recover \kfDualTwenty{} and \kfXattnTwenty{}.

Both networks were trained with the same recipe on the docking scores of known pockets and evaluated on the pockets of their training data against 10{,}000 ligands per pocket that were not used in training. Their checkpoint was chosen on seen pockets in five cross-fitted folds, which give every pocket four networks per condition, and the evaluation ligands were not used in this choice. A Jev-like variant with a learned readout in place of the product recovers \kfPooledTen{} (\kfPooledTenLo{} to \kfPooledTenHi{}) and keeps \kfRatioPooled{}\% of the recall of the cross-attention network (Table~\ref{tab:known}), so that the result does not depend on the product readout.

The choice of the checkpoint matters more for the cross-attention network than for the Jev-like network. When the checkpoint is chosen on new targets, the dual encoder and the cross-attention network recover \kpDualTen{} and \kpXattnTen{} on the \kpNTargets{} training pockets of the existing networks, against \kpConstTen{} for the constant pocket, and the existing networks, including the pooled variant, are within \kpSpreadTen{} of each other (Supporting Information). The cross-attention network stops after \kpXaBestLo{} to \kpXaBestHi{} updates with this rule, against \kfXaBestLo{} to \kfXaBestHi{} updates when the checkpoint is chosen on known pockets.
