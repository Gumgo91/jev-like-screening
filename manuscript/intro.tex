\section{Introduction}

Structure-based virtual screening ranks compounds by docking them into a protein pocket. Libraries of more than one hundred million molecules have been docked,\cite{lyu2019} an open-source platform has screened more than one billion compounds,\cite{gorgulla2020} and hierarchical schemes reach a library of eleven billion compounds by docking less than 0.1\% of it.\cite{sadybekov2022} Campaigns of this size are feasible on modest computer clusters,\cite{bender2021} multi-billion-scale collections of make-on-demand compounds are freely available,\cite{tingle2023} and the docking scores of the best molecules improve with the size of the library.\cite{lyu2023}

The cost of the docking calculation limits this scale. GPU engines accelerate AutoDock Vina\cite{vina,vina12} and AutoDock4,\cite{santosmartins2021} with reported speed-ups of more than 1{,}000-fold for Uni-Dock over one CPU core of AutoDock Vina\cite{unidock} and 65.6-fold on average for Vina-GPU 2.0 over AutoDock Vina in two DrugBank screens.\cite{vinagpu} Uni-Dock docks \dockRate{} to \dockRateFastest{} ligands per second on one GPU in our measurements, depending on the search mode, at 4{,}000 ligands per call, which still puts a billion molecules at \dockBillionDaysFast{} to \dockBillionDays{} GPU-days for a single pocket. Machine-learning surrogates reduce this cost for one target. Deep Docking, active learning and related workflows dock a sample of the library, fit a model to its scores and dock only the compounds that the model ranks first.\cite{deepdocking,gentile2022,molpal,yang2021,carlsson2025} On a library of 1.56 billion compounds, HASTEN recovered about 90\% of the 1{,}000 top-scoring molecules while docking 1\% of it,\cite{sivula2023} and regression-based active learning on libraries of one million molecules recovered up to 90\% of the top 1\% of the docking hits while docking 10\% of them.\cite{marin2024} Regressors on molecular fingerprints predict about 5{,}800 docking scores per second,\cite{berenger2021} and networks that emulate docking from the two-dimensional structure of the ligand are orders of magnitude faster than the docking program.\cite{jastrzebski2020} Benchmarks across targets and docking programs test the robustness of these protocols,\cite{difilippo2025} and reviews describe the field.\cite{sadybekov2023,cavasotto2023} Each of these models serves one target, so that the docked sample of the library is paid again for every pocket of interest.

A surrogate that is trained on the docking scores of many pockets can serve all of them with one network, and its cost depends on where the network lets the ligand and the pocket meet. Scoring functions of deep learning mostly let them meet early. Convolutional networks read a three-dimensional grid of the protein-ligand complex,\cite{ragoza2017,stepniewska2018,francoeur2020} GNINA scores docked poses with an ensemble of such networks,\cite{gnina} graph networks model the interactions between the atoms of the ligand and the protein in three-dimensional complexes,\cite{jiang2021,moon2022,lai2024} and TransformerCPI, a sequence-based predictor of compound-protein interaction, lets the atoms of the compound attend to the protein in a transformer decoder.\cite{chen2020} KarmaDock accelerates the docking of large libraries,\cite{zhang2023} and EquiScore reaches high accuracy on screening test sets.\cite{cao2024} In these joint networks the representation of the ligand depends on the pocket, and every ligand-pocket pair passes through the interaction layers.

Jev, a model from TypeSafe, evaluates many typed questions about one shared state in parallel and in isolation and returns all probabilities from a single query.\cite{jev,jevdocs} We found no published description of the architecture of Jev as of 10 October 2026. The public description fixes the shared state, the isolated evaluation of the questions and the single query without saying where a question meets the state (Figure~\ref{fig:concept}a). A surrogate that follows this organization encodes the pocket once into a state that stays in memory, embeds every ligand independently of the pocket and of the other ligands, and lets the ligand meet the pocket only at a cheap readout, so that a screen of a dual encoder against many pockets reduces to a product of two sets of vectors (Figure~\ref{fig:concept}b). We call such surrogates Jev-like. The joint counterpart lets every ligand atom attend to the residues of the pocket in cross-attention layers,\cite{vaswani2017} so that the representation of the ligand depends on the pocket and every ligand-pocket pair passes through the interaction layers (Figure~\ref{fig:concept}c).

Text retrieval knows the same choice. Cross-encoders that read a query and a document together are accurate but too slow to rank a large collection, and bi-encoders that embed both independently allow the embeddings of the collection to be computed once.\cite{humeau2020} Finding the most similar pair among 10{,}000 sentences takes about 65 hours with BERT as a cross-encoder and about 5 seconds with sentence embeddings.\cite{reimers2019} ColBERT, a late-interaction ranker, precomputes the document representations offline and runs about two orders of magnitude faster than BERT rankers at competitive effectiveness,\cite{khattab2020} and dense dual encoders outperform sparse BM25 retrieval in open-domain question answering.\cite{karpukhin2020} Dual encoders trail cross-attention models in accuracy,\cite{menon2022} and their fixed-length vectors limit the precision of retrieval.\cite{luan2021} Contrastive dual encoders also align images and text in a shared space for zero-shot transfer.\cite{radford2021}

Screening of protein-ligand pairs has taken this route. DrugCLIP aligns pocket and molecule embeddings in a shared space and turns screening into retrieval,\cite{drugclip_arxiv,drugclip} CoSP pretrains pocket and ligand representations with a contrastive loss,\cite{gao2023} LigUnity learns a shared pocket-ligand space,\cite{ligunity2025} and DrugHash stores contrastively learned binary hash codes of the molecules.\cite{han2025} Pocket-conditioned screeners report large speed-ups.\cite{drugclip,pharmaconet} DrugCLIP is reported to run up to ten million times faster than docking and scored about 500 million compounds against 10,000 human proteins,\cite{drugclip} and PharmacoNet scored 187 million compounds on a 32-core CPU in 21 hours and is reported to run 3,000 times faster than AutoDock Vina on PDBbind.\cite{pharmaconet} The joint network is the reference against which this speed is measured. It has the same encoders and sees the same ligand and pocket, and it adds the one element that the Jev-like organization leaves out, the exchange of information between the atoms of the ligand and the residues of the pocket before the readout. A comparison with the same encoders and the same training recipe therefore isolates the effect of the shared state. If the joint network recovers more of the top docking hits, the interaction layers buy accuracy with their cost, and if it does not, the screen has no use for them.

Machine-learning scoring functions that predict binding for targets outside their training data exploit the biases of their data sets and lose accuracy on proteins and ligands that differ from the training data,\cite{chen2019,yang2020,volkov2022} and the benchmark accuracy of models trained on PDBbind rests largely on leakage of similar complexes between the training and test sets.\cite{graber2025} DOCKSTRING provides the docking scores of 260,155 ligands against 58 targets,\cite{dockstring} a benchmark whose labels are computed for every ligand-pocket pair. The networks of this study serve the pockets of their training data, the setting of a screen for which a docked sample of the library exists for every pocket of interest.

We trained a dual encoder, a pooled-concatenation network and a cross-attention network with the same recipe on the docking scores of the training pockets of DOCKSTRING\cite{dockstring} (Figure~\ref{fig:concept}) and evaluated them on new ligands for the pockets of their training data. A network with one constant dummy pocket marks the recall that the ligand alone supports. On the known pockets the dual encoder, the pooled network and the cross-attention network hold \kfDualTen{}, \kfPooledTen{} and \kfXattnTen{} of the top-1\% docking hits in the best 10\% of the ranking, against \kfConstTen{} for the constant pocket, so that the Jev-like networks keep \kfRatioDual{} to \kfRatioPooled{}\% of the recall of the joint network. The model time of the cross-attention network for \scanLig{} ligands against \nPocketsBench{} pockets is \tmARatioXaHalfFiftySevenModel{} to \tmARatioXaSingleFiftySevenModel{} times that of the dual encoder, and \tmARatioXaHalfThousandModel{} to \tmARatioXaSingleThousandModel{} times against 1{,}000 pockets, measured on one \tmAGpu{}. The dual encoder ranks the \pocketPairs{} million pairs of the library against 57 pockets from SMILES in \tmADualFiftySevenFull{} s, and the docking of the same pairs takes \pocketDockDaysFast{} GPU-days in the fastest search mode of Uni-Dock.

(c) In the joint cross-attention network the ligand atoms attend to the residues of the pocket in interaction layers, so that each new pocket adds one set of residue states and one interaction pass per ligand.
